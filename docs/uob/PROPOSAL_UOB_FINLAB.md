# MIMI × UOB FinLab — Finding the affluent business owners banks cannot see

**Proposer:** MIMI Wallet (CTCP CLI Nutrix, Vietnam) · mimiwallet.online · Founder & CEO: Đinh Văn Nam
**Challenge:** Find the right affluent customers. Improve acquisition conversion.
**Focus areas covered:** 01 Affluent Prospect Intelligence · 02 Financial Needs & Life-Event Intelligence · 03 CVP Matching · 04 Next-Best-Acquisition Action

## 1. The segment UOB cannot see today

Vietnam has about 6.3 million business establishments, of which about 5.3 million are household businesses (2026 Economic Census). Many owners are becoming affluent, but a bank cannot tell: sales, loans and family transfers arrive mixed in one personal account, so neither balances nor statements show how much the business really earns. Since 1 January 2026 these owners must self-declare revenue, which pushes them to clean up their books — the moment MIMI enters.

UOB Vietnam has publicly set a goal of more than 1 million customers by 2029 with a focus on affluent and emerging-affluent clients. MIMI offers a source of **emerging-affluent business owners** that is invisible to traditional prospecting and requires **no UOB internal data**.

## 2. What MIMI already runs (market-ready)

MIMI is live in production (since 09/2026). With each owner's consent it reads their bank accounts via licensed open-banking partners (Casso/BankHub), separates real sales revenue from loans, capital and family money with evidence per line, drafts tax returns, runs a POS with VietQR auto-matching, forecasts 13-week cash flow, and builds loan files. It sells through accounting firms, whose staff manage many household clients in one MIMI workspace.

## 3. The solution: consented growth signals → needs → UOB CVPs → next best action

| Challenge area | What MIMI computes (deterministic, explainable, versioned) |
|---|---|
| **01 Prospect intelligence** | 11 business growth signals from cleaned cash flow: revenue already above or projected to cross ₫1bn / ₫3bn, sustained growth, new branch, rising payroll, large one-off capital inflows, loan disbursement and regular repayment, foreign-currency / Korea-linked flows, persistent surplus cash, seasonal working-capital gaps. Each signal carries evidence, confidence and a plain explanation. A business **growth tier**: Early growth → Rising → Ready to expand. |
| **02 Needs & life events** | Signals map to need categories: working capital, business account & cash management, FX / cross-border, deposits for surplus cash, premium banking for the owner. |
| **03 CVP matching** | A configurable offer catalog seeded with UOB Vietnam's public CVPs, each with its public source: Privilege Banking (≥ ₫1bn), PRVI Miles (income ≥ ₫35m/month, HCMC & Hanoi), business account + UOB Infinity, short-term credit & trade finance, FX, and the **UOB FDI Advisory Unit** for owners with Korea-linked flows. Unverified items never show thresholds. |
| **04 Next best acquisition action** | Channel and timing per need: through the owner's accounting firm, an in-app prompt right after a tax filing or a month before a projected threshold crossing, or an RM meeting **only when the owner requests it**. Ranked by signal confidence × need strength × channel fit. |

## 4. Privacy by design (Vietnam PDPL 2025, Decree 356/2025, Decree 91/2020)

- MIMI **never sells or shares lists of people**. UOB receives a referral only after the owner opts in to a specific offer.
- The consent screen discloses that MIMI analyses the owner's bank data to compute growth signals (profiling). No pre-ticked boxes; 7–90 day expiry; one-tap withdrawal deletes the package and keeps only a hash for audit.
- Default package: **growth tier + need categories only**. Exact revenue, name, tax ID or contact details are included only if the owner ticks them; contact details only if the owner asks to be contacted (no cold calls).
- Data goes only to UOB's Vietnam entity (no cross-border transfer).
- Not a credit score or credit information; no investment or insurance advice.
